# King of Tarts — single recipe page design inventory

Sources studied: the current King of Tarts homepage and the live Golden Pinenut Frangipane Tart page. The existing `design-tokens.md` remains the authority for type, colour, buttons, hover behaviour, spacing, and the 1140px content measure.

## Shared site elements

- Utility navigation: Home, About, Index, Membership, social links, and search.
- Doodle-backed logo band with the King of Tarts logo and “Baking Joy for Every Table” tagline.
- Primary navigation: Baking for Every Body, Recipes, Ingredient Swaps, Baking Tips, About, and Index.
- Footer: centred logo, Main Menu, Explore, static newsletter field/consent/button, and copyright.

## Homepage design language carried into the single page

- Playball for display headings.
- Roboto 18px/25px for readable body content and UI copy.
- Lora for navigation, metadata, labels, footer links, and editorial chrome.
- Mint `#9ECCBC` for primary actions, selected states, and soft surfaces.
- Amber `#EC9F2E` for categories, links, and emphasis.
- Cream `#FFFDD0` for supporting editorial callouts.
- 1140px centred content measure with full-bleed section backgrounds.
- Pill buttons and the existing mint/white hover reversal.

## Live single-recipe elements retained

- Breadcrumb categories.
- Recipe title, publication date, and author.
- Featured image.
- Jump to Recipe, Jump to Video, Print Recipe, and sharing actions.
- Editorial introduction and baking-science explanation.
- Recipe summary with preparation time, cooking time, total time, servings, course, and cuisine.
- Ingredients, equipment, method, video, and notes.
- Step-by-step preparation guidance.
- Dietary and ingredient swaps.
- Common mistakes and baker’s tips.
- Shelf-life and storage guidance.
- Frequently asked questions.
- Tags, related recipes, and a comment area.
- The same static newsletter footer used by the archive.

## UX enhancements

- A sticky “On this page” rail on wide screens.
- Accessible tabs inside the recipe card to reduce scrolling without removing content.
- Clear visual separation between editorial content, the practical recipe, science, and substitutions.
- Responsive two-column and single-column states.
- Reading progress, print styling, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support.
